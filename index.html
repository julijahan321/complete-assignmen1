Dr. Muhammad Yunus, a Bangladeshi social entrepreneur and economist, is the founder of the Grameen Bank and a pioneer of microfinance. 2006, Dr. Yunus and the Grameen Bank were jointly awarded the Nobel Peace Prize for their efforts to promote economic and social development. He firmly believes that poverty is not created by the poor but by the structures of society that limit opportunities. Dr. Yunus envisions a world where businesses serve humanity through social impact rather than profit maximization. His work continues to inspire initiatives worldwide that combine financial inclusion and social entrepreneurship to create lasting change.
